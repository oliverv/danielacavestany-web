Workshop · Creative iPhone photography experience

Learning to see


An invitation to pause, observe and discover the hidden beauty of the everyday. You don't need a professional camera. The tool is the iPhone; the true camera is the gaze.


FormatDetail
Duration3 hours
GroupMaximum 8–10 people
PlaceBarcelona — Born, Gràcia, Poblenou, Montjuïc, Barceloneta
Price60–120 € per person depending on format

Program

I

Opening the eyes

30 min · Welcome coffee


What makes an image move us? The difference between looking and seeing. Photography as a form of presence.

Exercise: photograph something you would normally walk past.

II

The walk of the gaze

1 h 30 min · Photographic exercises


1. Light. Seeking how light transforms the simple: a wall, a window, a shadow, a flower.

2. Invisible details. Building a collection: textures, colours, small gestures, objects with a history.

3. Emotion. One photograph answering: what do I want someone to feel when they see it?

III

A small story

45 min · Selection and narrative


Each person selects 5 images: my small tale of Barcelona. Working on order, rhythm and the connection between images. Sharing them with the group.

Method

Learning to see before photographing


The photographic eye: training attention and finding beauty in the everyday.

Light and atmosphere: how a scene changes with the hour and the emotion.

Intuitive composition: balance, emptiness, colours, lines, textures.

Visual narrative: creating a story with 5–10 images.

Sensitive editing: not transforming reality, but enhancing it.

iPhone tools we will master: exposure, focus, AE/AF lock, HDR, portrait and night modes; rule of thirds, symmetries, layers; editing in Apple Photos, Lightroom Mobile and Snapseed.

What you will learn

✓ To use the iPhone with creative intent
✓ To find beauty in the everyday
✓ To compose images with emotion
✓ To create your own visual narrative
✓ To trust your own gaze


"For years I have created images for magazines, spaces and brands. But my true search has always been another: learning to see. This experience is born from the need to rediscover beauty in the simple, to stop the noise and find stories in places where apparently nothing happens."
