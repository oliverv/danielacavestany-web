01

Learning to see

Creative iPhone photography experience · 3 hours · 8–10 people · Barcelona


An invitation to pause, observe and discover the hidden beauty of the everyday. Part one — Opening the eyes (30 min): a welcome coffee, what makes an image move us, the difference between looking and seeing. Part two — The walk of the gaze (1 h 30 min): light transforming the simple, invisible details, emotion. Part three — A small story (45 min): each person selects 5 images, their small tale of Barcelona.

You will learn to use the iPhone with creative intent, to compose with emotion and to trust your own gaze.
