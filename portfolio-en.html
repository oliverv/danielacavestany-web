Bali — Land and Offering


Intimate portraits, temple rituals and landscapes of jungle and sea. Each image tries to capture the beauty of what I find there: the eyes of those people, so clean; their natural smiles; the temples, the palms, the rains; lives so precarious and, at once, so full.
