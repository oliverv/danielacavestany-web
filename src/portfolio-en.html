Photographing the jungle Bali

Series: Bali

I don't photograph the extraordinary, but what we usually overlook.

This series of photographs comes from my life in Bali, the island where I moved with my children for a few years. There I found silence, spirituality and a simpler life surrounded by nature.

My photographs are of the nature that embraced me, the landscapes I discovered in the silence, and the Balinese people who made me feel welcome.

Their life in the temples, the rituals, the smiles and the beauty of the offerings.

I photographed the black-sand beaches, the sea that watched my children grow up surfing among the waves, and the rice fields that accompanied us at home.

This is my view of Bali: what moves me, what inspires me.
