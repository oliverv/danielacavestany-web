Bali — Land and Offering

I am Daniela Cavestany, interior designer and photographer. My art is born from observing and finding beauty in the simple: moments, people, landscapes and small details that often go unnoticed.

This series of photographs comes from my life in Bali, the island where I moved with my children for a few years. There I found silence, spirituality, nature and a simpler life.

My photographs are of the nature that embraced me, of the landscapes I discovered in the silence, and of the Balinese people who made me feel welcome. Their life in the temples, the rituals, the smiles, and the beauty of the offerings.

I photographed the black-sand beaches, the sea that watched my children grow up surfing among the waves, the rice fields that kept us company at home.

This is my gaze on Bali: what moves me, what inspires me, and what I have carried to the deepest part of my soul.

Because everything goes, except what you remember forever, what stays with you or stays in a photograph.
