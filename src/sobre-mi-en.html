DANIELA CAVESTANY

Photographer · Interior designer

Photographer · Interior designer

Graduated in Art Direction and Photography from the London College for the Applied Arts and trained in photography at GrisArt. She began her career in art direction and graphic design, collaborating with various agencies and creative studios.

For twenty years she worked as a decoration stylist, developing a special sensitivity to spaces, materials, light and small details. That experience is now part of her work as an interior designer.

Her artistic vision brings together photography and interior design, two disciplines that share a search for beauty, balance and authenticity. Through photography she explores the relationship between light, emotion and memory, creating poetic and timeless images.

Biography

My story

My story is deeply tied to the journey back and forth between East and West. I was born in Barcelona, into a bourgeois family. My great-great-uncle was the architect Enrique Sagnier, and that connection to the city was very present in my family. But from childhood I felt I belonged to Asia, where my mother was born, in the Philippines, and where my mixed roots originated.

I remember the smell of Asia as I stepped off the plane for the first time, that mixture of humidity and heat that still makes me feel at home. There I discovered another way of living, seeing and feeling; a freedom that would stay with me throughout my life.

For twenty years I worked as a decoration stylist, a profession that allowed me to develop my sensitivity to spaces, light, materials and small details. Photography has always been part of that way of observing and understanding beauty.

Many years later, I took my children to live in Bali. I wanted them to discover that other world, to grow up between two cultures, as if I could give them the same feeling of belonging that I had discovered as a child.

It was my mother who introduced me to Formentera during my childhood, without imagining that many years later I would build a house there where I would raise my children close to nature. That is how Casa Daniela Formentera was born, a very personal project in which I have been able to express my understanding of spaces and life. All those experiences have shaped the way I see, feel and find beauty in the simple. That sensitivity remains present today in both my photography and my interior design projects.

What makes me different

My images seek to be a refuge of beauty, calm and authenticity.
